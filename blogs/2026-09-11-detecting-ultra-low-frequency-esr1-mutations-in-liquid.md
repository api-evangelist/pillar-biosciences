The oncoReveal® Essential LBx Panel is a single-tube, 34-gene amplicon-based NGS liquid biopsy assay built on SLIMamp® chemistry. Pillar tested it on Seraseq® ctDNA ESR1 reference standards diluted to 0.05% variant allele frequency (VAF) from 5–30 ng of plasma cell-free DNA (cfDNA), sequenced on the Illumina NextSeq 550 and analyzed with PiVAT® software. It detected these endocrine-therapy-resistance mutations in HR-positive breast cancer with 98.4% PPA at 0.1% VAF and 100% NPA — no molecular barcodes (UMIs) required.
